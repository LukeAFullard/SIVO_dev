# Tracking *E. coli* in the Whanganui FMU

We regularly monitor *E. coli* levels year-round to track our environmental progress and help keep the community safe. The spatial map shows the current *E. coli* state grades across the Whanganui FMU. These A-to-E grades represent the estimated risk of sickness from faecal bacteria that might be in the water at each local monitoring site.
