# The Rangitīkei-Turakina FMU

The Rangitīkei-Turakina River is unique—it is the only river in New Zealand that completely crosses a mountain range. It begins on the eastern side of the Ruahine Ranges, cuts through the stunning Te Āpiti (Rangitīkei-Turakina Gorge), and flows across the plains before finally meeting the Tasman Sea at Foxton Beach.

According to Māori tradition, the river's name tells a story. When the explorer Haunui-a-Nanaia reached its wide, cold waters, it made him catch his breath. In te reo Māori, *manawa* means breath, and *tū* means to stand still.
