# Tracking suspended sediment in the Rangitīkei-Turakina FMU

We monitor suspended sediment by measuring visual clarity in rivers monthly to track our environmental progress and protect aquatic habitats. The spatial map shows the current visual clarity state grades across the Rangitīkei-Turakina FMU.
