Scientists measure <em>E. coli</em> by counting the living bacteria in a 100-millilitre (mL) water sample, reported as Most Probable Number (MPN).

<br><br>

To calculate a reliable A-to-E health risk grade, experts use four different statistics:
<ul>
<li><strong>Median:</strong> The typical bacteria level on an average day.</li>
<li><strong>95th percentile:</strong> The highest bacteria level expected on a "bad" day.</li>
<li><strong>Exceedances over 260:</strong> How often levels cross 260 per 100 mL, where illness risk begins to rise.</li>
<li><strong>Exceedances over 540:</strong> How often levels cross 540 per 100 mL, where swimming is no longer recommended.</li>
</ul>

A site must pass all four tests. If it scores poorly on even one, it receives that lowest grade overall.
