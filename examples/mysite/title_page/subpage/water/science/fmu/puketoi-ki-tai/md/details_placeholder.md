# The Puketoi ki Tai FMU

The Puketoi ki Tai Freshwater Management Unit (FMU) is the least populated in our region, characterised by a remote and rugged landscape. It is formed by three main rivers—the Ākitio, Wainui, and Ōwahanga—that flow from their headwaters high in the Puketoi Range out to the Pacific Ocean on the east coast. 

The name *Puketoi ki Tai* translates to "from the range to the coast," perfectly capturing the journey of these waterways. Along the way, the rivers carve through pastoral farmland and highly erodible hill country, featuring stunning natural landmarks like the spectacular Waihi Falls.
