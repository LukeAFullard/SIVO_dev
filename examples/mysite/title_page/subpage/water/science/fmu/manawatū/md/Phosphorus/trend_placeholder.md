# Long-Term Trends for Dissolved Reactive Phosphorus

### Understanding the Trend
Trends show us if DRP levels are consistently changing over time. We use a 10-year timeframe to see past temporary weather spikes—such as heavy rain washing extra fertiliser into the river—and uncover the true, long-term direction of water quality.

### Trend Categories
Trends are grouped by how confident scientists are that a change is actually happening. Because excess DRP fuels harmful algal blooms, an "improvement" means the concentration of DRP in the water is safely decreasing.

*   **Improving:** DRP levels are consistently decreasing (*very likely* or *likely improving*).
*   **Degrading:** DRP levels are consistently increasing, posing a higher risk to aquatic life (*very likely* or *likely degrading*).
*   **Indeterminate:** There is not enough statistical evidence to confirm a direction.
