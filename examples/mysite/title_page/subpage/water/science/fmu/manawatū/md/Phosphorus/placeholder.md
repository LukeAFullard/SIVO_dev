# Tracking phosphorus in the Manawatū FMU

While Phosphorus usually attaches to soil, it can also dissolve in water. This dissolved form, known as Dissolved reactive phosphorus (DRP), is easily absorbed by aquatic plants and algae.
We regularly monitor DRP levels to track our environmental progress and protect our freshwater ecosystems. The spatial map shows the current DRP state grades across the Manawatū FMU.
