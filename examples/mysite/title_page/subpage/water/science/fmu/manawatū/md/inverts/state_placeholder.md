### How We Measure and Grade
Scientists collect these small animals from the riverbed, usually once a year, and identify the different species present to calculate a score called the Macroinvertebrate Community Index (MCI). This index is based on the known sensitivity or tolerance of each species to pollution. 

To calculate a reliable A-to-D ecological health grade, experts use the **median** (middle) MCI score from data collected over a five-year period. 

*   **Band A:** Represents pristine, healthy conditions where sensitive insects thrive, indicating almost no organic pollution or nutrient enrichment.
*   **Band D:** Represents severe pollution, where sensitive insects have been lost and the community is largely composed of hardy, pollution-tolerant species like snails and worms.
