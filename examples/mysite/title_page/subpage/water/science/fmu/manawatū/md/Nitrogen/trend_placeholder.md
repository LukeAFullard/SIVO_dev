### Understanding the Trend
Trends show us if nitrogen levels are consistently changing over time. We use a 10-year timeframe to see past temporary weather spikes and uncover the true, long-term direction of water quality.

### Trend Categories
Trends are grouped by how confident scientists are that a change is actually happening. Because nitrogen can be toxic to aquatic life, an "improvement" means the concentration of nitrogen in the water is safely decreasing.

*   **Improving:** Nitrogen levels are consistently decreasing (*very likely* or *likely improving*).
*   **Degrading:** Nitrogen levels are consistently increasing, posing a higher toxic risk to aquatic life (*very likely* or *likely degrading*).
*   **Indeterminate:** There is not enough statistical evidence to confirm a direction.
