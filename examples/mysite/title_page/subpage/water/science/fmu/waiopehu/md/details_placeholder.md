# The Waiopehu FMU

The Waiopehu Freshwater Management Unit (FMU) encompasses a diverse range of lakes, wetlands, rivers, and streams, including the Ōhau and Waikawa Rivers. Freshwater in this unit makes its journey from the peaks and foothills of the Tararua Ranges, flowing over the rich soils of river terraces and plains before reaching the Tasman Sea. Along the coast, stable dunes extend inland, with beautiful wetlands and lakes nestled between them.

A prominent feature of the Waiopehu FMU is Te Moana o Punahau (Lake Horowhenua). As a shallow coastal dune lake, it is the largest natural lake in the Manawatū-Whanganui Region. It receives water from several streams, drains, and groundwater, and has a single outflow to the sea via the Hōkio Stream.
