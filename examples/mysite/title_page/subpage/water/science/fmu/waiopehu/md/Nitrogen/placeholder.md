# Tracking nitrogen in the Waiopehu

These two forms of nitrogen below are directly toxic to aquatic life at high concentrations. They enter our waterways in different ways:
*   **Ammoniacal nitrogen:** This form primarily reaches rivers through direct point-source discharges, such as treated wastewater or dairy shed effluent.
*   **Nitrate nitrogen:** This form is highly soluble and typically washes into waterways from the land. It leaches downward through the soil into groundwater and rivers, often coming from excess fertiliser and animal urine patches.

We regularly monitor these nitrogen levels to track our environmental progress and protect our freshwater ecosystems. The spatial map shows the current toxicity state grades for both nitrate and ammoniacal nitrogen across the Waiopehu FMU.
