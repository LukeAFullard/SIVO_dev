# Damp firewood 

Burning wet, or green (unseasoned) firewood can emit more gases and smoke that carry small particles into the air that people can breathe in. 

To avoid this, check the moisture content of firewood before purchasing it, especially if you plan to use it within six months. You can request this information from your firewood supplier, or test your woodpile at home using a moisture meter, which can be purchased from your local hardware store 

For best burning results, it is recommended to use firewood with a moisture content of less than 25%.
