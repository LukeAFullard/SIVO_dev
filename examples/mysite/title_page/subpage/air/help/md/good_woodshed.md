# Store firewood correctly 

Buy or collect your firewood early: the earlier you buy wood, the more cost-effective it will be, and the longer it will have to season. 

Split, stack, and store your firewood correctly by: 
* Splitting wood into 15cm thick pieces before storing it, as logs dry faster when split.
* Stacking wood in a loose criss-cross pattern in a dry, covered spot off the ground to let air circulate.
* Storing freshly cut wood for 6-12 months to allow it to season, to ensure it burns cleanly and effectively.
