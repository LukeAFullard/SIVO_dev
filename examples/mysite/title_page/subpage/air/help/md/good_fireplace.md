# Cleaner and NES-compliant heating appliances

If possible, install or upgrade to a National Environmental Standard (NES) compliant wood burner, or choose a cleaner form of heating, such as a heat pump.  

Check out the EECA’s website to see if you are eligible for a Warmer Kiwi Homes grant. Warmer Kiwi Homes offers grants of 50%-90% off insulation and up to 90% off heating for eligible households. 

[Warmer Kiwi Homes — Insulation and heater grants | EECA ](https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/information/?utm_source=301&utm_medium=htaccess)
