## Home heating 

Home heating appliances like wood burners, especially when operated incorrectly, can emit smoke, which carries particulate matter into the air. 

Results from Horizons’ last air emissions inventory for Taumarunui and Taihape (2010) indicate domestic home heating is the main source of PM<sub>10</sub> emissions in these towns, accounting for 84% and 82% of daily wintertime emissions, respectively.
