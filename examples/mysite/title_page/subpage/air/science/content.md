Horizons Environmental Data team measures the concentrations (amount) of PM10 and PM2.5 at the region’s two gazetted airsheds - Taumarunui and Taihape.

An airshed is a legally defined geographic area for air quality management. When these areas were gazetted, air quality was known to breach the National Environmental Standards for Air Quality (Air Quality NES) in these towns. These standards set the minimum level of air quality required to protect New Zealanders’ health.

Greater concentrations of particulate matter in the air indicate poorer air quality.
