# Manawatū

The Manawatū FMU is a patchwork of landscapes that includes pastoral farming on the open plains, lowlands and upland hill country. Extensive native forest covers parts of the Ruahine and Tararua Ranges. Urban centres include Palmerston North, Feilding, Dannevirke and Foxton, and smaller villages are scattered across the FMU.
