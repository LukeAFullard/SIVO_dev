# Whanganui

The Whanganui FMU contains extensive areas of native forest. The remaining parts are predominantly pasture with some exotic forestry. Main urban centres include Taumarunui and Whanganui, with many smaller rural settlements.
The Whanganui River channel has high sided cliffs of sand and mudstone. Much of the land surrounding the awa is hill country made of the same material. This hill country and land structure is prone to erosion which can lead to excessive sediment or mud entering the waterways.
