At Horizons Regional Council, we understand and appreciate how important our soils are to our region and its people. We initiated a long term hill country erosion-control program – Sustainable Land Use Initiative (SLUI) after our region witnessed the impact of devastating floods and resulting landslides and soil erosion, in 2004.
It is a largest, non-regulatory soil conservation program running successfully for almost two decade, in the country. The primary objective of the program is to improve the resilience of soil and our land, particularly in the hill country. It is an example of a successful collaboration between landowners and the regional council, in the country.
