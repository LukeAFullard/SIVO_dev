Landowners and farmers are the heroes of the program. Without there willingness, support and contribution it would have been impossible to run this program. About 41% of total funding in year 2024-25 for SLUI came from the landowners. If you are a landowner- get in touch with Horizons and our amazing team can assist in developing farm plans under SLUI. 

Even if you are not a landowner, you are already contributing to the program by paying your rates. 

There are several small yet meaningful actions that you can do to preserve our whenua and conserve our soils.  These include practicing 3Rs - *recycle, reuse and  reduce* or 3Rs, supporting sustainable farming practice by understand and learning where your products come from and understanding kind of practices employed by the farmers.

You can also participate in community activities such as stream clean-ups, tree planting, and kai support initiatives, or even do something as simple as mulching your backyard.
