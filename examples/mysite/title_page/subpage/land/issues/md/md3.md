Soil erosion doesn’t happen in silos, meaning it not just a matter of loss of topsoil layer but influences larger ecosystem. Soil erosion often leads to slips and landslides, loss of topsoil influences nutrients and agricultural production which in turn affects economic benefits. In addition, it clogs the waterways and affects hard infrastructure such as roads, dams and bridges, which requires substantial capital investment and are vital to our daily activities. Soil run off into pollutes the water ways and harm the aquatic life. Therefore, it is important to look at soil and soil erosion from a holistic perspective and not a local phenomenon.
