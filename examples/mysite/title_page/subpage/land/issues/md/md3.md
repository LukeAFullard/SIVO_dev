Erosion of soil is not just a matter of loss of top soil layer, soil erosion influences larger ecosystem. The soil erosion often leads to slips and landslides, loss of top soil influences nutrients and agricultural production which in turn affects economic benefits. In addition, it clogs the waterways, hampers hard infrastructure such as roads, dams and bridges, which are requires a large of amount of capital investment and forms an important lifeline for our day to day lives. Soil run off into streams causes nutrient loss but also, pollutes the water ways and harm the aquatic life. Therefore, it is important to look at soil and soil erosion from a holistic perspective and not a local phenomenon.
