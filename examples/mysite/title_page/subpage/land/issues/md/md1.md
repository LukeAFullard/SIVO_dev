We dedicate, 2025-26 Annual SOE report, to **our soils.** We all know that soil, particularly, topsoil stores nutrients for plants and is important for food production, healthy soils make healthy bodies. Function of soil is not limited to supporting food-crops. Soil filters water that seeps into the ground and replenishes ground water, an important source of fresh water for drinking and other uses. A whole ecosystem of microbes lives in the soil that thrives on organic matter contributing to carbon storage. Surprisingly, as per latest studies (Nature 2005), soil is the largest terrestrial carbon sink, more than what atmosphere and vegetation can hold combined. Soil is a powerhouse of various ecosystem goods and services.
