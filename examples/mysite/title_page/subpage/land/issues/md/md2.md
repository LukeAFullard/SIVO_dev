Soil in our region is very fertile supporting different ecosystem services and goods and various agricultural and economic activities including dairy farms, forestry etc. Our region is naturally susceptible to soil erosion, for it largely being a hill country (fragile geology), prone to floods and storms (weather events) and supporting largest area of hill country farming in NZ (human activities). Rainfall plays a key role in washing away the top soil with it. The characteristics of land itself such as soil type, slope, weather events such as excessive rainfall, and storms contributes to soil erosion. However, human activities such as clearing land for food production, converting land for settlements, urban infrastructure and intensive farming practices influences the health of soil and intensifies soil erosion.  Climate change is like to further exacerbate the rate of soil erosion in the Horizons Region.
