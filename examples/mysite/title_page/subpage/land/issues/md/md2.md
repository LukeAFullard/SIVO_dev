Soil in our region is very fertile supporting various agricultural and economic activities including dairy farms, forestry etc. On the other hand, our region is naturally susceptible to soil erosion, for it largely being a hill country (fragile geology) and prone to frequent rainfall, floods and storms (weather events). Rainfall and extreme weather events along with characteristics of land itself such as soil type, slope contributes to the soil erosion which is further accelerated by human activities such removal of tree cover, over grazing, construction of hard infrastructure such as roads, bridges and dams, and other land-use changes. Climate Change is going to further exacerbate the soil erosion and other environmental degradation in general.
